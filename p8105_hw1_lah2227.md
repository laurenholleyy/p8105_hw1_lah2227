### Problem 2: Creating a Data Frame

`log_var` returns a mean because the `TRUE` and `FALSE` values are
treated numerically as either 0 or 1. `char_var` returns `NA` due to the
fact that it is not numerical or logical, and stores letters as its
values. `fact_var` also returns `NA` due to its non numerical or logical
nature. It stores its values as numbers but doesn’t treat them as
numeric.

Testing out `as.numeric`

``` r
variable_df |>
  pull(log_var) |>
  as.numeric()

variable_df |>
  pull(char_var) |>
  as.numeric()

variable_df |>
  pull(fact_var) |>
  as.numeric()
```

`char_var` cannot be converted to numerical due to the current letter
nature of its values. `fact_var` needed to be converted into its
underlying factor category codes from its current character categories,
which is why you couldn’t initially take the mean.
